5-STEP START GUIDE

Bybit, screen by screen
just follow along

Jump to the step you need. Just press the orange boxes in order.

Go to the sign-up page →

This button opens the sign-up page with a 20% fee cashback already applied.

check the code

  1. 1Enter your email.
    The verification code goes to this address.
  2. 2A green check next to code 167951 means the discount is on.

  3. 3The terms box is already ticked.

Sign up 2Bybit02

Choose
your country

  1. 1Under Where do you live? pick your country.
    It's usually filled in already.
  2. 2Check the terms box and press Create Account.
    If a puzzle appears, slide the piece into place.
Your screen may look a little different

Bybit updates its layout now and then. The button names in this guide are the ones shown on screen, so follow those.

Sign up 3Bybit03

Enter
the email code

  1. 1A 6-digit code arrives by email.
    Enter it within 5 minutes.
  2. 2Fill the six boxes and it moves on by itself.
  3. 3No email? Check your spam folder first.
    When the timer runs out, use Resend.
Sign up 4Bybit04

Create
a password

  1. 1Type a login password in Password.
  2. 28–30 characters with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number.
    Once all four rules are ticked, you're good.
  3. 3Press Create Account and you're signed up.
Don't reuse a password from another site

An exchange account holds real money,
so give it a password you use nowhere else.

Security 1Bybit05

Open
the Security page

  1. 1Profile icon (top right) › Account › Security on the left.
    Your sign-up email is already turned on.
  2. 2Press Settings on the Google Two Factor Authentication row.
    Once linked, it shows a green dot like here.
Finish this before you deposit

Withdrawals need both an email code and an authenticator code.
Changing the authenticator later locks withdrawals for 24 hours.

Security 2Bybit06

Confirm it's you
with an email code

  1. 1Settings opens a Security Verification window
    and sends a code to your email.
  2. 2Enter the 6-digit code from the email.
  3. 3No code? Request it again when the timer on the right ends.
Install an authenticator app first

Get Google Authenticator (free) from the App Store or Google Play.
Microsoft Authenticator works too.

Security 3Bybit07

Scan the QR,
enter the code

  1. 1In the authenticator app, tap + › Scan a QR code.
    The QR sits in the grey box shown here.
  2. 2Can't scan? Type in the key from Copy key.
    Write the key down on paper.
  3. 3Type the app's 6 digits into Google 2FA Code, then Confirm.
    It changes every 30 seconds.
Never screenshot this QR or key

Anyone with it can make your codes.
On a new phone, restore it from the written key.

Identity 1Bybit08

Start
identity verification

  1. 1Profile icon › Account › Verify Now under Identity Verification.
    Pick the country that issued your ID.
  2. 2Pick your ID under Document Type.
    Driver license · ID Card · Passport · Residence permit.
  3. 3The orange button at the bottom takes you to the photo step.
Deposits and futures unlock after verification

New accounts can't deposit crypto or trade perpetuals
until the first level (Standard) is verified.

Identity 2Bybit09

Photograph
your ID and face

  1. 1Have the physical ID, a bright spot and your face ready.
    Photo files and scans aren't accepted.
  2. 2Start uses your computer's camera.
  3. 3No camera on your computer? Choose Use mobile phone.
    Scan the QR with your phone and continue there.
ID requirements

It must be clear and readable, not expired,
and you must be 18 or older.

Identity 3Bybit10

Check
you're verified

  1. 1When the review is done it shows Identity verified · Standard.
    Usually 15 minutes, up to 48 hours.
  2. 2The name in Personal Info should match the account you send from.
    Exchanges compare names on larger transfers (Travel Rule).
Nothing to do while it's reviewed

You'll get an email and a notification when it's done.
Use the time to finish your security setup.

Deposit 4Bybit14

Choose
the Tron network

  1. 1Select chain opens the network list.
  2. 2Click TRON (TRC20).
  3. 3On the right: it arrives after 20 confirmations,
    and deposits under 0.005 USDT won't be credited.
Choose TRON on the sending side too

If the sending and receiving networks differ,
the coins are lost and can't be recovered.

Deposit 5Bybit15

Copy the address,
pick the receiving account

  1. 1Copy it with the button next to the address
    and paste it into the withdrawal screen you send from.
  2. 2Route Deposits To is the account the coins land in.
    The default is Funding.
  3. 3Switch it to Unified Trading
    and you can trade without a transfer.
Never type the address by hand

Always use the copy button. One wrong character and the money is gone.

Deposit 8Bybit18

Move funds
to the trading account

If you set the receiving account to Unified Trading earlier, skip this.

  1. 1Assets › Transfer.
    Funding → Unified Trading is the right direction.
  2. 2Coin: USDT.
  3. 3Enter the Amount (All = everything) and press Confirm.
    It moves instantly with no fee.
Futures 1Bybit19

Open
the futures screen

  1. 1Hover over Trade in the top menu.
  2. 2Hover over Futures; the markets appear on the right.
    Keep the USDT tab selected.
  3. 3Click BTCUSDT to open Bitcoin futures.
    The address should start with bybit.com/trade/usdt.
Don't mix it up with Spot

Spot is for buying coins outright.
This guide uses the Futures screen.

Futures 2Bybit20

Pick
a market

  1. 1Click ☰ BTCUSDT at the top left
    to open the market list.
  2. 2Type a coin in the search box,
    using tickers like BTC, ETH or SOL.
  3. 3Click a row and the screen switches to it.
    Perpetual means a futures contract with no expiry.
Start with big markets like BTC or ETH

High volume means orders fill easily and prices jump less.

Futures 3Bybit21

Set margin mode
and leverage

  1. 1Click Cross to open the list.
    Choose Isolated.
  2. 2Click 10.00x for the leverage list.
    Pick one or set your own with Customize.
Isolated vs Cross

Isolated can only lose the money in that position. Cross puts your whole balance on the line, so a big move can wipe it out. The default is Cross · 10x.

Futures 4Bybit22

Order type,
price and amount

  1. 1Limit waits for your price; Market fills at the current price.
    The third tab holds advanced orders — skip it at first.
  2. 2Price is your price for a limit order.
    Last fills in the current price.
  3. 3Quantity is the size. Drag the 0–100% slider
    to use a share of your balance.
Market is the easy one at first

It fills instantly and needs no price.
The trade-off: the fill can be slightly worse.

Futures 5Bybit23

Long or short,
with take-profit and stop-loss

  1. 1Tick TP/SL and two fields appear below.
  2. 2Take Profit is your profit target; Stop Loss caps your loss.
  3. 3Long if you expect a rise, Short if you expect a fall.
    Filled orders show in the Positions tab below.
Always set a stop-loss

Leverage liquidates fast when price turns against you.
Add an SL price to every order.
